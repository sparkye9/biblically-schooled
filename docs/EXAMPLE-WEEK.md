- **Math** (15 min · Hands-on learning · nothing to print): Number Bonds to 5
  - Objective: Break numbers to 5 into two parts.
  - Warm-up: Show 4 on a ten frame in your mind: how many more make 10?
  - What: Breaking numbers up to 5 into two parts.
  - Why: A whole can be split into parts, and the parts put back together make the same whole. 5 is 2 and 3, and it is also 4 and 1. Knowing the parts makes adding and subtracting easy.
  - How: Start with the whole. → Split it into two groups. → Say: "__ and __ make __."
  - Say it: "5 is 2 and 3. 2 and 3 make 5."
  - Stage: Concrete — Hold a stick of cubes for the whole; snap it into two parts in different ways.
  - Teach: Say what we are doing: "Breaking numbers up to 5 into two parts." / 5 counters. Put 2 on one plate, 3 on the other. "5 is 2 and 3." / Say why it works: "A whole can be split into parts, and the parts put back together make the same whole. 5 is 2 and 3, and it is also 4 and 1. Knowing the parts makes adding and subtracting easy."
  - Practice: Find all ways to make 5. / Child explains one answer: "I know because…" while showing it with the objects.
  - Materials: Counters (beans, LEGO, cereal, or buttons), Two plates
  - Check: Finds 3 ways to make 5.
- **Handwriting** (5 min · Hands-on learning · nothing to print): L l W w · 2, 5
  - Objective: See and feel how L l W w and 2, 5 are formed.
  - What: Forming L, l, W, w and the numbers 2, 5.
  - Why: Starting each letter and number in the same place, moving the same way every time, makes writing faster and neater — and it keeps b/d and 2/5 from flipping.
  - How: Find the starting place (top line or middle line). → Say the formation words while you move. → Check: does it sit on the baseline?
  - Stage: Big movements — Sky-write with a straight arm, then finger-write in the tray. No pencil today.
  - Teach: Model slowly, saying the words as you go: / • L: Down and across. / • l: Down from the top. / • W: Slant down, up, down, up. / • w: Slant down, up, down, up. / • 2: Curve around, slant down, across. / • 5: Down, around the belly. Hat on top.
  - Practice: Sky-write each one 3 times with a straight arm. / Finger-write each one in the tray, then shake to erase.
  - Materials: Salt, sugar, or shaving-cream tray (a baking sheet works), Whiteboard or chalkboard
  - Check: Starts at the correct place without reminders.
