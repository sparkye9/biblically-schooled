# Scope & Sequence — 36 Weeks

_Generated from `lib/curriculum/*` by `npm run docs:curriculum`. Do not edit by hand._

Each week has four lesson days: **Introduce → Practice → Apply → Review**. Wednesday is co-op by default; lessons only advance when school actually happens.

## Curriculum map

- **Unit 1 (Weeks 1–9): God, Creation, and Identity** — God made everything, He is good, and He made me on purpose in His image.
- **Unit 2 (Weeks 10–18): Faith, Covenant, and God's Promises** — God makes promises and always keeps them — we can trust Him.
- **Unit 3 (Weeks 19–27): Jesus Christ and Salvation** — Jesus is God’s promised Savior who lived, died, and rose again for us.
- **Unit 4 (Weeks 28–36): Christian Living and Spiritual Growth** — God’s Spirit helps us pray, love, and serve as we grow.
