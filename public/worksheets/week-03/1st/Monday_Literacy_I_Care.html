Sight Words & Blending: I Care
Week 3 • Monday • 1st Grade • Literacy • Reading Fluency
Activity 1: Sight Word - I
The word I is special! It's a sight word. You see it in books all the time!
I
The letter I all by itself = the word I
Activity 2: Blending Words with /C/
Blend these words. Say the sounds, then say the whole word.
/K/ - are = CARE
/K/ - an = CAN
Say each word 3 times: CARE, CARE, CARE. CAN, CAN, CAN.
Activity 3: Read the Sentences
Read each sentence out loud. Draw a line to the matching picture.
I care.
I can.
I can care.
