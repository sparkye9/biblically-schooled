Our story.

Cassia was born from a simple belief: that body care should be as intentional as your skincare. We started small, whipping batches in our Frederick, Maryland kitchen because we wanted to create something that felt like a small luxury, not a rushed step.

Every jar is made with real ingredients we'd put on our own skin. We believe in transparency, quality, and formulas that work with your skin's natural biology.
