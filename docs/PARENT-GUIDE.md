# Start teaching in two minutes

1. **Open the app.** It opens on **Today**: the date, which lesson day you're on, and every child's work.
2. **Pick the kind of day:** Full (60–90 min), Standard (40–50), Minimum (~20) — or tap **I need an easy day** for a 20-minute, no-printing plan. Switching never loses anything.
3. **Glance at "What you need"** and **"How to run it"** (who you teach while the others work alone).
4. **Bible together**, then each child. Tap **How to teach** for a script: Objective, Materials, Teach, Practice, Check, Next step.
5. **Check the big boxes** as you go. Seraiah and Amelia share one card with separate boxes.
6. **Tap "We're done for today."** Unchecked items simply stay unchecked — nothing piles up.

## Good to know
- **Missed days don't skip lessons.** The next lesson waits for you. After time away, Today offers **We're starting again**.
- **Print My Week** (Print Center): one click prints the overview, daily checklists, every worksheet (two named Pre-K copies), and a separate answer key. Choose "Save as PDF" to keep a file.
- **Progress** is by skill. You choose: Not introduced, Introduced, Practicing, Demonstrated independently, Needs review.
- **Students**: put a child on a different level or pace per subject, and pick learning-support reminders.
- **Kid view**: big buttons, read-aloud, stars. Leaving it needs a grown-up (PIN or a math question).
- **The weekly rhythm**: Monday (after church) is a hands-on day with nothing to print · Tuesday is the paper day · Wednesday is co-op · Thursday is hands-on and movement · Friday is review and a short mastery check, plus science and library. Every lesson shows a label: Academic, Hands-on, Movement, Discussion, or Review. Change days in Settings.
- **Math** always shows *What · Why · How*: objects first (Monday), then pictures and written problems (Tuesday), real life and movement (Thursday), and an independent check (Friday). Timed practice only appears after you mark the concept "Demonstrated independently."
- **Students → What the curriculum adds**: handwriting (on for Pre-K and K), language building, and access supports, per child. These change how lessons are delivered, never the grade level.
- **Video day**: on a day you need a break, tap **I need an easy day → Videos for this week** (or This Week → Video day). Each child gets videos matched to that week's Bible story, letters, math, and science from vetted channels (Saddleback Kids, Suitcase Teachers, Jack Hartmann, Numberblocks, SciShow Kids). Preview a minute of anything new and turn off autoplay.
- **Deactivate** a child (Students) or a whole household (Settings → Households) to hide them everywhere without deleting anything. Amelia and Damilola's homeschool are deactivated; reactivate them from Students → Deactivated.
- **Back up** now and then: Settings → Backup.
